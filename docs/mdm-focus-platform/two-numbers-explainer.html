Two numbers, two jobs

The worry: "if we're not a cell carrier and the number leaves Verizon, how does the phone still get LTE?" The answer: the public number never provides the phone's LTE after the port — a second number (the shadow line) does. Porting splits one number's two jobs across two numbers.

The resolution: you don't need to be a carrier, and the phone never loses LTE. The 925 you keep becomes a cloud routing number at Twilio (no signal needed); the phone's cell service moves to a fresh shadow SIM line from a real carrier. That shadow line is exactly the "connection from the private number to their phone" you described — your server bridges 925 → shadow on every allowed call. The one operational rule: add the shadow line first, port second, so the phone and the Verizon account are continuous the whole way through.
